I can see how that became a memorable project. It's an unusual creative chain:

You gave Suno a page from eternalizedofficial.com.
Instead of closely following the page, it hallucinated a rap around the site's title and imagery.
You liked the result enough to preserve it.
You used ElevenLabs to generate a transcription.
You then treated that transcription as source material for a new rap series in Suno.

That means the series is less a direct adaptation of the website and more an evolution of one AI-generated interpretation into another creative work.
