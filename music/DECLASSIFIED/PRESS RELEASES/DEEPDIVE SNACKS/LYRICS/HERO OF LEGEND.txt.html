Long ago, there lived a young man,

In a green hood, who hunted through the land,

killing all the monsters that threatened the villagers,

He was celebrated in renown as a hero of heroes.

He heard the tales of an evil king,

in the background masterminding everything,

and he found and killed the evil monster king,

OUR HERO IS VICTORIOUS they'd sing and sing

and they crowned him with a crown as king,

making law, deciding everything,

and reign did he for many years as king

and very well he decided everything

and they sang

SAY HELLO TO THE GOOD LIGHT

I'M NOT EVEN A LITTLE AFRAID TO DIE

SAY HELLO TO THE MOONLIGHT TONIGHT

and grew he to hate their cats

and his men looked like monsters instead

and every now and then he drew in another mother

and even young men and merchants were assailed with one another

there wasn't anywhere safe anymore, there wasn't anywhere safe there.

it was all because he hated them

he wished that he could say that to them

why he dragged away their cats and mothers every other day

and every odd day someone's brother, and a father falling too here and there

before the villagers knew it,

they didn't live in a village at all

they were more like pumpkins

sitting on someone's farm
