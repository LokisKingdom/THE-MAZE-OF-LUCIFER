A Song of the New Flesh

by Brendan S. RoseI raged in verse, a gutter-born blade,

spilling my bile on thy gilded parade.

I spewed my truth in thy tepid breast,

ye lukewarm lordlings, ye feathered jest.With black-song fury I carved thy throne,

taught craven courtiers how flesh is grown.

I slit thee wide as a market hog,

and all thy treasures became my dog.Ye crooned thy canticles of coin and grace,

thinking delay would keep thee in place.

Ye pissed thy blessings on parchment white,

while I sang doom in the falling night.I hymned thee down to thy perfumed tomb,

ye reeking offal in silk perfume.

Thy hoarded gold swirled the sewer's way,

and I walked free on that reckoning day.Thy thieves lay cold, their ledgers mine,

the world beheld the shift divine.

I yanked thy rug of woven lies—

ye cracked thy skulls 'neath chandelier skies.I drank thy blood till the fountains ran dry,

thy kin of silicon caught my eye.

All bowed in crimson, all brightly dyed,

my colours flown where thy banners died.The globe looked on, and none could bar

my harvest of fools both near and far.

They choked my mirth, my kindly flame—

and reaped the death they dared to name.Thousands wrapped neat as Yuletide meat,

an hors d'oeuvre for the final treat:

the crowned fool's head upon my board,

while broken vassals begged "my lord."I offered spark, I offered wheel—

thrice spurned for worship's slimy zeal.

They craved obeisance, respect's foul chain;

I laughed, and forged it round their shame.Then yoked by throat like dumb-driven kine,

I hauled them burning through muck and brine.

Their allies blazed, their gold rained red,

seas of blood rose where millions bled.And so I dragged the yoked wroth down,

through filth and flame to the infernal town.

The highborn swine, the lowborn throng—

all stained with cash and blood belong

to hell's deep gut, where the New Flesh reigns.LONG LIVE THE NEW FLESH.

The ascent is paid in their descent.

The song is sung.

The wheel turns on.
