The design philosophy I'd keep in mind is:

Every new mechanic should interact with as many existing mechanics as possible.

If you add a barrel, ask:

Can it burn?
Can it explode?
Can it be pushed?
Can it fall?
Can it block doors?
Can a black hole move it?
Can a teleporter move it?
Can monsters attack through it?

Each "yes" multiplies the number of possible situations.

The nice thing is that you don't need hundreds of unique items. Fifty items that all interact with one another can produce vastly more interesting gameplay than five hundred isolated items that each do only one thing.
