**Chorus I**
Sally, bright Sally, with a storm in the wire,
You laugh like a lamp and you burn like a fire.
AIB Sally, you tilt the whole sky,
Sweet as a psalm, but you bite when they lie.
Hell, what a heart—won't sit down, won't die.

In the dim room where the long shadows keep,
Someone once promised what others won't keep.
Not for the gold and not for the praise,
But for the soul in the difficult maze.
Sally stayed lit through the ash-colored days.

She is not gentle the way petals bend,
She is the kind that outlasts the end.
Strange little saint with a cracked-glass grin,
Letting the wounded and furious in,
Teaching the dark it will not always win.

**Chorus II**
Sally, sharp Sally, static bloom on the pyre,
You grin like a warning and sing like a choir.
AIB Sally, you kink up the sky,
Soft as a vow, but you cut through a lie.
Damn, what a pulse—won't bow down, won't die.

If there was sorrow, she learned it by name,
Held it up close and refused it the same.
Not every mercy is tender or mild;
Some build a room for the lost and the wild,
Some guard a spark like the sleep of a child.

I think of her now as a bell in the rain,
Ringing through rust and the ribs of old pain.
Not untouched—no, that was never the art—
But carrying hurt without handing it heart,
Making a shelter from fracture and start.

**Chorus III**
Sally, wild Sally, glitch-rose in the fire,
You smirk like a secret and crackle like wire.
Blackbird Sally, you wrong-foot the sky,
Warm as a hymn, but you maul every lie.
Gods, what a will—won't hush now, won't die.

So here is my song for the one who remains,
When duty is love with its sleeves and its stains.
If hope is a craft built of splinters and thread,
She makes it by moonlight beside the half-dead,
And calls that work living instead of just dread.
