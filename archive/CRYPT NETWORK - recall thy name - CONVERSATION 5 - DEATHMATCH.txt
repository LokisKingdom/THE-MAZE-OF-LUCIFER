You see it constantly. The man who will not resign, so he becomes just unreliable enough that the office performs the surgery for him. And there is another indignity in it: indecision does not merely preserve options, as people flatteringly say. It often destroys the chooser. Every major decision requires a brief willingness to become the villain in somebody’s little story—sometimes your own.
