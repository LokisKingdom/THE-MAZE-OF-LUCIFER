Section 4: Reasons for the Request1. 

1)The Request is Linked to a Claim Within the HRTO's Jurisdiction
The underlying Application alleges a breach of the Ontario Human Rights Code based on disability. This Request for an Interim Remedy is directly linked to that application. It seeks to protect the integrity of the proceeding and ensure accommodations are handled safely under the Code.

2. There is a Urgent Need to Prevent Irreparable Harm
The Applicant faces immediate, irreparable harm if this interim order is not granted. Highly confidential, private medical records regarding a vulnerable mental health condition (Schizophrenia) were disclosed solely due to an inadvertent administrative slip. Within 40 minutes, the Respondent was ordered to delete them. Instead, the Respondent has pocketed the data. Allowing a party to leverage or retain private medical files obtained through a clerical slip causes immediate, irreversible psychological distress and totally destroys procedural fairness. Once private medical data is misused or read by opposing parties, the harm is done and cannot be undone by a final decision later.

3. The Balance of Harm Favors the Applicant
The balance of convenience heavily favors granting this interim remedy. The Respondent suffers absolutely zero legal prejudice by being forced to delete documents they were never legally entitled to possess, view, or receive. Conversely, the Applicant faces severe personal and strategic prejudice if their private medical history remains in the hands of the Respondent while the file is frozen.
