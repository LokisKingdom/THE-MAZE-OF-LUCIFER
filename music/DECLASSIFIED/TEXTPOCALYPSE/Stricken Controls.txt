========================================================
--------------------STRICKEN CONTROLS-------------------
========================================================

WSAD to move selector Up/Down/Left/Right
Q/E to move selector forward and backward along the Z Axis

T and G to change your selected piece (Clicking The Pieces On The Sides Will Also Do This)

K for next song
L for random song

P to change the Skybox

Backspace to cycle through selector modes

Arrow Keys to move camera forward, backward, left, and right

[You may also move the camera with the numpad]
Numpad 8(forward)/5(backward)/4(left)/6(right)
Numpad 9(move camera vertically upward)
Numpad 7(move camera vertically downward)

Numpad:
/ * - + . 0
(Rotate Pieces On The Board)
Numpad enter:
(Return Pieces To Normal)

Hold Left Click to Rotate the Camera

========================================================
----------------KINGDOM OF LIES CONTROLS----------------
========================================================
WSAD to move.
Mouse to Aim.
Left Click to Attack.
Right Click to Change Weapons.

Escape to exit out of the level,
or to quit the game if you are in the main menu.

========================================================
--------------INVASION OF STUPID CONTROLS---------------
========================================================

Mouse to Aim.
Left Click to Attack.
T and G to Change Weapon.
Backspace to Upgrade Weapon.

First Weapon Upgrade costs 100000 points.
Second Weapon Upgrade costs 300000 points.

Escape to exit out of the level.
