WITHOUT PREJUDICE — SETTLEMENT TERMS

To Michele Warner, Counsel for CAMH

Currently Cited Cases: 2026-67461-I (HATE CRIMES UNIT) (passed service) 2026-68857-I (CAMH)(pre-service) 2026-68924-I (ODSP PARKDALE : POLITICAL)(pre-service) 2026-69006-I (EMAN AND PATRICK NEWLAND)(pre-service) 2026-67449-I (Soundcloud is not cited, as it is off the table unless negotiated)

1. Parties and cases

These terms apply, case-by-case, to each proceeding, complaint, application, or pre-service claim presently active against CAMH and/or its servants, agents, or employees in which the undersigned is claimant/applicant (“each Active Case”).

CAMH is the paying party. Counsel is not a party.

2. Money (non-negotiable opening position)

CAMH shall pay CAD $100,000.00 per Active Case by certified funds or solicitor’s trust transfer so that the funds have cleared in the receiving account before that Active Case is withdrawn, and in any event within 14 days of full execution of that case’s minutes. The undersigned shall withdraw / discontinue that Active Case only after cleared payment. Withdrawal is not due, and no release operates, until the money has cleared. If cleared payment has been received and the undersigned fails to withdraw that Active Case within 14 days of clearance, CAMH may retrieve that $100,000.00 for that case. Retrieval is limited to the settlement sum paid on that case. It does not revive unpaid NDAs, future-claims bars, or any other covenant. Interest and thrown-away costs already earned stay with the undersigned unless a court or tribunal orders otherwise. If CAMH does not clear payment within 14 days of execution, the minutes are void at the undersigned’s election, the Active Case remains live, and CAMH shall pay $5,000 thrown-away costs on that case.

3. No bundled future-claims bar

A release, covenant not to sue, or bar on future claims is not included in the $100,000.

Any deal that would prevent, limit, or condition future proceedings against CAMH or related persons must be separately negotiated and separately priced. The undersigned is open to that negotiation. Silence is not consent.

4. Default release (narrow)

For the $100,000 on an Active Case only: full and final release of that Active Case and the facts pleaded in it, up to the date of the minutes.

No release of: future conduct; unknown claims arising after the minutes; Human Rights Code, AODA, PHIPA, or other statutory rights in respect of new acts; or claims against individuals not named as released in that instrument.

5. Confidentiality / non-disparagement

No NDA and no non-disparagement is granted by default.

If CAMH wants a mutual confidentiality covenant and a mutual non-disparagement covenant for an Active Case, CAMH shall pay an additional CAD $50,000.00 per Active Case for those two covenants together.

Any such covenants shall be mutual, equal in scope, and equal in duration. One-sided clauses are rejected.

No “non-admission” recital may be used to imply the undersigned recants facts.

6. Mental Health Act override (hard condition)

If, after execution, CAMH or anyone acting for it causes, seeks, supports, or relies on apprehension, detention, examination, or involuntary status of the undersigned under the Mental Health Act (Ontario) or equivalent:

every confidentiality and non-disparagement covenant in these settlements is void ab initio;

every bar on future proceedings in any related instrument is void;

CAMH has no right to repayment, clawback, restitution, or set-off of any money already paid;

the undersigned is free to commence new proceedings on any available cause, including in respect of that apprehension/detention and in respect of matters previously compromised only by those voided bars.

The money already paid is earned and stays with the undersigned.

The parties record that community stability and living well, not institutionalization, is the intended outcome of settlement.

7. No admission; no treatment leverage

Settlement is without admission of liability.

CAMH shall not use settlement talks, these terms, or payment as a basis to alter, condition, or withhold clinically indicated care, or to initiate MHA process.

8. Tax and characterization

Each $100,000 is paid as damages for injury to dignity, feelings, and self-respect and related non-pecuniary loss, unless the undersigned later agrees in writing to a different split. CAMH issues any T4A or equivalent only if legally required and only after 7 days’ written notice of the proposed characterization.

9. Default and enforcement

Missed payment date: the compromise on that case lapses at the undersigned’s option; all claims revive; and CAMH shall pay $5,000 liquidated costs for that default, in addition to the original claim.

These minutes may be converted to a consent order / judgment on 5 days’ notice.

10. Entire deal per case

No side letters. No “standard hospital release” appended later. Handwritten or extra schedules are void unless signed by the undersigned.

Governing law: Ontario. Forum: Ontario courts or the tribunal seized of that Active Case.

11. Open to negotiate quantum and structure

The undersigned will consider: timing of payment, number of Active Cases covered, and a separately priced future-claims package.

The undersigned will not consider: unpaid NDAs, one-way non-disparagement, clawback on MHA use, or bundling a future-suit ban into the $100,000.
