========================================================
STATES
========================================================

1: Menu State

The player is in his apartment, if he uses the toilet he quits
the game, if he uses the Stricken Cube he is teleported to the
Stricken Main Menu, if he talks to the rat he opens the front
door after a short story sequence, if he leaves through the
front door he is teleported to the next level, or rather to
the story segment for the next level, if he opens the fridge
he is teleported to the level select screen. If he plays the
game console he is transported to Invasion of Stupid.

The Room will become messier and messier as the game progresses
until it is a complete warzone, after which it will begin to warp
in strange and unnatural ways.

The Rat will slowly rot as the game progresses, until by the end
it is just a skeleton covered in jelly.

Although this is not extant in this build, the bathroom will also
deteriorate as the game progresses.

2: The Stricken Cube [4D CHESS]

The Player is in the Stricken Main Menu, from here you can start a game,
which I would encourage you to do because the game is at least as good
as Kingdom of Lies itself. It is my favorite board game. From the Stricken
Menu you can change various Stricken parameters, which will have an impact
on the game. As of now, a Black Score at 20 or above will give the player
extra weapons, a White Score of 20 or above will spawn a few new enemies,
an even score between the two will result in a huge amount of new enemies.
If I have time I would like to make a large amount of hidden content based
on the menu choices, all of these features will be hidden from the player and
will only be discovered through experimentation. Exiting Stricken will return
the player to the Kingdom of Lies Menu State.

3: The Story Sequence

Press any button to advance to the next story bubble, the game displays story objects
until it runs out, then loads the next level. An extensive story is planned, I would
also like the story bubbles to be usable mid level in a later build.

4: The Game Sequence

The player is in a level, usually a large house, the player uses one of three weapons
to kill everybody in the house, then exits via walking outside the bounds of the level.
You can return to the Menu State by pressing escape twice, a message will appear to confirm.
Move with WSAD and change direction with the mouse. Left Click to attack, Right Click to change
weapons. If a melee enemy gets too close to the player spawn point, they will die, but if an
enemy spawn camps you with a gun, too bad, you lose. The game functions on a one hit kill system,
if the player dies 7 times he is returned to the Stricken Main Menu.

I like the way the movement system works, the character has weight, and there's momentum.
I think the controls are pretty good, and the gameplay is pretty fun.

5: The Level Select Screen

By accessing the fridge, the player will open the level select screen.
Levels unlock as the player progresses. Click a button to go to that chapter.

6: Invasion of Stupid

Fight against wave after wave of social justice warriors in a hopeless
attempt to survive. This will be developed over the course of the project.

7: Alpha Resurrection

I have completely ported over Alpha Resurrection from Renpy into Fungus in Unity.
Now that it is completed, my next goal will be to finish both games.
I have currently completed one half of one path in AR. There are 3.
As of right now, it has 18 endings, with choices that are actually
meaningful to the story. Alpha Resurrection will be the True Ending of
Kingdom of Lies, but for now, I'm putting it in Chapter 3, top left TV.
