Introduction / Statement of Facts
Private Prosecution Application – Brendan S. Rose v. Sam Suttle
This application concerns events that occurred between 2021 and early 2023 while Sam Suttle exercised significant financial control over my living situation as Power of Attorney for my mother.

In 2021, Sam Suttle’s actions, including multiple forced relocations while my wife was pregnant, contributed significantly to the breakdown of my marriage.
In the summer of 2022, Sam Suttle had my pets removed and disposed of, leaving me almost entirely isolated except for one cat.

From approximately mid-2022 onward, while I was living at Kingston Road, Sam Suttle imposed a monthly rent of $650 on my ODSP income of approximately $1,000–$1,100, plus additional bills and credit card debt. This left me with roughly $150 per month for food. Despite my repeated requests for additional funds for basic necessities, these requests were denied or ignored. Sam Suttle eventually blocked me from calls and later used my desperate emails against me.

During this same period, I became unable to attend the clinic to renew my psychiatric medication due to lack of bus fare and extreme physical weakness. I effectively went without medication.

By October 2022, the combination of isolation, loss of my pets, and severe food deprivation caused a profound physical and mental decline. I engaged in self-harm, resulting in significant blood loss throughout the apartment. Videos from this period document the conditions I was living in.

In November 2022, the situation continued to deteriorate. On or around November 18 and 19, 2022, as a result of extreme hunger and mental breakdown, I was involved in violent incidents that led to my arrest. I was taken to Scarborough Centenary Hospital on November 19, 2022, where I was in a state of severe distress and extreme hunger. Medical staff provided immediate food after I had been screaming for it for hours. I required hospitalization.

I believe that but for the extreme conditions imposed and maintained by Sam Suttle, this level of deterioration would not have occurred.

In early 2023, I was able to secure new housing only days before Sam Suttle’s deadline that would have left me homeless in that weakened state

I believe Sam Suttle’s conduct demonstrated a wanton or reckless disregard for my life and safety. I am seeking charges of criminal negligence causing bodily harm (s. 221) and aggravated assault (s. 268).The evidence in support includes email correspondence, audio recordings, graphic photographs and videos, and medical records.
