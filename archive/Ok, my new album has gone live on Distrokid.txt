Ok, my new album has gone live on Distrokid.

It's: Art Therapy For Latent Criminals

For Album 1 I played it safe, I gave 2 fire songs, and a whole bunch of staple soup,
which i started to regret longterm. So today I just snapped and decided i was gonna
release a new album with all the stuff i was too scared to post before,
This album is all fire. No fat. I'm very happy with this one.
And the other was good too, they compliment each other.
And they Published A Warm Place.

They published everything, swallowed the poison pill expecting more.
I was surprised to learn i don't have as many albums as i thought this year.
Turns out in the last 7 months I've made the equivalent of 2 really good albums.
That's a very human sounding output. compared to the 9000 songs i'm always talking about.

I also like the male and female versions of Art Therapy For Latent Criminals,
because it totally changes the character of the song depending on who's singing,
what sounds like an abusive husband at first, transmogrifies into a woman
taking the house and kicking the husband out while she physically
and psychologically abuses him and threatens to tear out his hair.
"when you get kicked out of your address" yeah it takes on a different
kind of threatening character when coming from a woman.
But the man plays his role very well too, overall I couldn't be more
delighted with my first 2 test runs of Distrokid, now I'll build albums
slowly, one every month or two. And I'll build a huge collection. Badass!!!!!
