Dear Soundcloud Legal,
RE: Brendan Samuel Rose v. Soundcloud Inc
HRTO File No: 2026-67265-I
Please find attached, by way of formal service pursuant to the Human Rights Tribunal of Ontario (HRTO) Rules of Procedure, the following documents:
1. HRTO Form 10 – Request for Order During Proceedings (including evidence exhibits)
2. HRTO Form 23 – Statement of Delivery
Context and Nature of the Request
As documented in the attached Form 10, an administrative error occurred on 2026-05-28 whereby highly confidential, unredacted personal medical files were inadvertently forwarded to your attention. A mere forty (40) minutes later, I issued an urgent, explicit written demand requesting that your office immediately delete and purge this information.
To date, your office has pocketed this sensitive data, refused or failed to confirm its destruction, and appears intent on retaining records you have no legal entitlement to possess.
Procedural and Substantive Impact
Please be advised that your retention of this data causes direct, severe, and negative impacts on my personal privacy and psychological well-being. Furthermore, attempting to leverage or retain private medical files obtained strictly through an obvious clerical slip introduces an acute abuse of process into these proceedings.
This conduct severely compromises the integrity of this forum and directly threatens procedural fairness. A party cannot exploit an inadvertent disclosure to gain an unfair evidentiary advantage, especially while the Tribunal file is frozen.
Notice to the Respondent
Pursuant to HRTO Rules, you have fourteen (14) days from the date of this service to file a Form 11 Response to this Request with the Tribunal and deliver a copy to me.
Should you choose to permanently delete and purge all copies of the inadvertently disclosed medical files—and confirm this destruction to me in writing prior to that deadline—I will promptly advise the Tribunal that the matter is resolved.
Sincerely,
