Willowbrook was the kind of place where time seemed to
linger, where the days flowed at a leisurely pace, and where the
weight of the world felt a little lighter on its residents'
shoulders. It was the quintessential small town, a haven of
tranquility nestled amidst rolling hills and canopied forests,

wildflowers in the spring and the fiery foliage of autumn.
On the surface, Willowbrook appeared to be a town straight
out of a storybook. Its residents knew one another by name,
and everyone greeted each other with warm smiles on the
cobblestone streets. The old town square featured a towering
oak tree, its branches offering shade to weary travelers, and a
serene pond where children fed the ducks on lazy summer
afternoons.
But beneath this facade of serenity, beneath the welcoming
smiles and friendly waves, Willowbrook concealed a sinister
secret—an enigma that had plagued the town for decades. It
was a secret whispered about only in hushed tones, shared
among close friends and family, and only when the sun dipped
below the horizon, casting long shadows over the town.
For generations, Willowbrook had been the home of St. Jude
Hospital, a sprawling institution that had stood as a pillar of
the community since time immemorial. Its imposing white
façade glistened in the daylight, and its red brick chimneys
billowed plumes of smoke, signaling life within. The hospital

14
had earned a reputation as a place of healing, a sanctuary for

counterpart. Whispers in the darkest corners of town spoke of
unspeakable horrors that unfolded within the hospital's walls.
Patients who entered seeking treatment were said to emerge,
if they emerged at all, bearing the weight of inexplicable
suffering. Families who had entrusted their loved ones to the
care of St. Jude sometimes found their beloved kin altered
beyond recognition, their minds and bodies twisted by an
unseen malevolence.
Willowbrook's shadowy secret was encapsulated in the
whispered tales of St. Jude Hospital, a sister institution

people sick. It was a paradox that had confounded the town for
generations—a riddle with no solution and a darkness that
would soon cast its long shadow over the lives of those who
dared to confront it.
As twilight settled over Willowbrook, the town seemed to hold
its breath, as if it knew that the horrors of St. Jude were
